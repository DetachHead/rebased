Reports unindented blockquote continuation lines that rely on lazy continuation without an explicit > marker.

Lazy continuation reduces plain-text readability, makes refactoring prone to syntax errors (such as breaking blockquotes on blank lines), and can lead to inconsistent rendering in Markdown parsers.

Example:

> Line 1 of blockquote
Line 2 of sample text

After applying the quick fix:

> Line 1 of blockquote
> Line 2 of sample text
